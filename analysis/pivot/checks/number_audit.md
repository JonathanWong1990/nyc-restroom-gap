## Five-line summary
1. One outright error: **"9 open 24h" should be 8**, and those 8 are parkway gas stations. "Structural quadruples" is **wrong for the headline pool (3.2×)**; only the seven-inspector subset reaches ~4.8×.
2. **8.5% → 20.1% is arithmetically exact and statistically solid** (+11.6 pp, CI 7.8–15.3). But 2024 is the **lowest year since 2005**, so the slide should say "~9% flat 2022–24, 20% in 2026, highest since 2012". The litter/graffiti placebo holds but sits near 1%, so it is weak on its own.
3. **0.788 vs 0.703 = Spearman rank correlation** (how well each method orders held-out neighbourhoods by complaint count, averaged over 200 splits). It reproduces, but should be shown as 0.79 vs 0.70 and described as predicting complaints, not need.
4. **The 21-at-6pm figure assumes Parks restrooms close at 4pm**. With an 8pm in-season close it is 9. The 4 at 2pm and 43 at 9pm hold in both scenarios. $4.0M is a band midpoint (exact-dollar median $3.8M vs $1.37M).
5. All pivot figures reproduce (48.9/79.1/86.1/88, 11.7 vs 20.2, 17.8%, 67→68). The 67→68 comes from a smaller 254-slot matched subset, and the 20.2% is a different measure from the Turn's 20.1%. "Cheapest lever" and the 1 Nov deadline are **UNVERIFIED**.
