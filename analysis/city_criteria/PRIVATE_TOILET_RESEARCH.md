# Private toilets: background research (research agent, 28 Sep 2026)

Question for Q&A: "Don't Starbucks / McDonald's already provide restrooms, so the gap is smaller?"

## Findings (with confidence)
- **Starbucks**: open-restroom policy for anyone, May 2018; **reversed 27 Jan 2025**: code of conduct requires a purchase to
  stay or use facilities, ~11,000 North American company-owned stores. High confidence.
  NPR 14 Jan 2025 https://www.npr.org/2025/01/14/g-s1-42699/starbucks-bathroom-reversal-what-to-know-open-door ;
  CBS News https://www.cbsnews.com/news/starbucks-open-door-policy-reversal-purchase-now-required/ ;
  UPI https://www.upi.com/Top_News/US/2025/01/14/Starbucks-purchase-policy/6391736860515/
- **McDonald's**: no corporate restroom policy; franchisees decide (spokesperson quoted by 24/7 Wall St, 2023). Moderate.
  https://247wallst.com/special-report/2023/01/29/mcdonalds-bathroom-inspections/
- **NYC keypad codes / Dunkin' policy**: not verified. OPEN.
- **Law**: New York's Restroom Access Act covers only customers with documented medical conditions (probable; secondary
  sources). NYC code requires customer toilets only in restaurants with 20+ seats (high confidence;
  https://portal.311.nyc.gov/article/?kanumber=KA-01351). No general public right to use a business's toilet.
- **Behaviour**: YouGov (2022, ~2,000 US adults): 44% have bought something solely to use a restroom.
  https://today.yougov.com/society/articles/45245-toilet-talk-polling-americans-potty-preferences
  Crohn's & Colitis Foundation survey via Crain's op-ed (14 Jul 2023): 54% of New Yorkers say not enough public restrooms
  nearby (moderate; source blocked, summary only). https://www.crainsnewyork.com/op-ed/new-york-businesses-need-incentives-open-their-restrooms-public
- **Pay-to-open schemes**: London Community Toilet Scheme (from Richmond 2005; about half of London boroughs; ~£500–£1,200
  per business per year). Age UK report 2022 https://www.ageuk.org.uk/bp-assets/globalassets/london/documents/campaigns/community-toilet-schemes-in-london---report-by-margit-physant.pdf
  Germany "Nette Toilette" (since 2000, ~210 cities, ~$34–$112/month per business). Bloomberg 1 Nov 2016
  https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2016-11-01/germany-s-nice-toilet-network-delivers-cheap-public-restrooms
- **NYC**: Council press release on LL58 (10 Apr 2025) lists private partnerships with commercial establishments as one
  route; no operating NYC pay-to-open scheme found. https://council.nyc.gov/press/2025/04/10/2831/

## Safe slide lines
- Starbucks' open-restroom policy ended in January 2025; a purchase is now required.
- No enforceable right to a free restroom at NYC businesses; McDonald's leaves it to franchisees.
- 44% of Americans have bought something just to use a restroom: businesses are a paid, unreliable stopgap.
- Cities that rely on business toilets (London, German cities) pay for access; NYC has no such scheme yet.
